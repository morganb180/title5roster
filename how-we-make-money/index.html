How we make money

Title 5 Roster is free to use. Here is how it is, and will be, paid for, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

On this page

  1. Today
  2. Planned
  3. Our rules

Today

  • Nothing is for sale. Every listing is free and built from official public records.
  • Listings are ordered by location and name. No one can pay to appear higher, to get a license badge, or to have a competitor removed.

Planned

  • Featured placements, clearly labeled "Ad", shown alongside, never instead of, the full free list.
  • Quote requests sent to approved inspectors near you, if we add them, will be free for buyers and sellers. Inspectors may pay a per-request fee; that never changes who is listed or the order of the free list.

Our rules

  • Paid or not, every listing must match a current official record.
  • Guides and cost pages are written independently of advertisers and cite their sources.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a link, the link will say so.

Professionals: see claim or correct your listing.

Disclaimer: Inspector listings come from the MassDEP-approved System Inspector lists published by NEIWPCC (lists current as of 10/2/2026, retrieved 2026-10-12). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and this site is not affiliated with MassDEP or NEIWPCC. Confirm an inspector's approval before hiring. General information, not legal advice.
