## Frequently asked questions

**How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Suffolk County?**

Title 5 Roster lists 5 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors (5 listings) with an address in 2 towns in Suffolk County, from NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability.

**Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Suffolk County?**

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before a sale (3 years with annual pumping), and the report goes to the local Board of Health ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/buying-or-selling-property-with-a-septic-system)). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the town's Board of Health.

**Which Suffolk County towns have the most approved inspectors?**
