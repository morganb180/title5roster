Title 5 septic inspectors in Berkshire County, MA

Quick answer: Title 5 Roster lists 56 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors (55 listings) with an address in 24 towns in Berkshire County, from NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region. Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before a sale (3 years with annual pumping), and the report goes to the local Board of Health.
