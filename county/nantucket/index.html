Frequently asked questions

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Nantucket County?

Title 5 Roster lists 10 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors (10 listings) with an address in 1 town in Nantucket County, from NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability.

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Nantucket County?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before a sale (3 years with annual pumping), and the report goes to the local Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the town's Board of Health.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Nantucket County?

We don't have county-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.
