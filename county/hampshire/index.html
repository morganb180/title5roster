Frequently asked questions

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Hampshire County?

Title 5 Roster lists 34 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors (34 listings) with an address in 11 towns in Hampshire County, from NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability.

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Hampshire County?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before a sale (3 years with annual pumping), and the report goes to the local Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the town's Board of Health.

Which Hampshire County towns have the most approved inspectors?

Southampton (7 listings); Granby (6 listings); Belchertown (4 listings). Counts are listings with an address in each town on NEIWPCC's list.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Hampshire County?

We don't have county-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.
