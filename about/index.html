About Title 5 Roster

Title 5 Roster is a free directory of MassDEP-approved Title 5 septic System Inspectors in Massachusetts, organized by town, with plain-language guides to septic inspections at property transfer.

It is for Massachusetts home buyers, sellers, agents and attorneys who need a Title 5 inspection, and for inspectors who want to claim or correct their listing. Listings come from NEIWPCC's public lists of MassDEP-approved System Inspectors (current 10/2/2026). Inspectors employed by town boards of health or MassDEP are not listed as hireable.

Where the information comes from

  • Inspector listings: NEIWPCC's lists of MassDEP-approved System Inspectors.
  • Rules: 310 CMR 15.000 (Title 5) and MassDEP's buying or selling property with a septic system guide.
  • Costs: published price guides and Mass.gov tax-credit and loan pages, cited on the cost page.

How it's built

  • Listings are rebuilt from the official source files on each refresh; every listing shows its source and retrieval date.
  • Rules and cost guides link the statute, regulation, agency page or official fee schedule they rely on. Where we couldn't confirm something from an official source, we say so or leave it out.
  • Nothing here is legal advice. Requirements change, so confirm with the agency or town before you act.

Who operates Title 5 Roster

Title 5 Roster (title5roster.com) is operated by Title 5 Roster. Contact us through the contact page.

Independence

Title 5 Roster is independent. It isn't affiliated with any government agency, licensing board or contractor, and a listing is not an endorsement. See how we make money, how we verify listings and about our data.
