# Title 5 Roster: find a MassDEP-approved Title 5 septic inspector in Massachusetts

Title 5 Roster is a free directory of MassDEP-approved Title 5 septic System Inspectors in Massachusetts, organized by town. Every listing shows the SI number from the NEIWPCC list. The quote form connects you with up to 3 nearby approved inspectors. They contact you directly.

1,139 approved System Inspectors in 1,104 listings, from NEIWPCC's list of MassDEP-approved inspectors (current 10/2/2026). Inspectors employed by boards of health or MassDEP are left out because they generally act as regulators, not private hires.

Search by town or ZIP opens the matching listing. It does not start a quote request.

The homepage sample is labeled "Highly rated on Google." It includes only inspectors rated 4.7 or higher with at least 20 Google reviews, retrieved October 30, 2026. It is a sample, not the directory order. Town and county listings stay ordered by location and name.
