Get free quotes from up to 3 approved Title 5 inspectors

Tell us what you need and the ZIP code of the property. We send your request to up to 3 MassDEP-approved Title 5 System Inspectors near you, and email you their names. They contact you directly. It's free, and there's no obligation. Available for Massachusetts ZIP codes.

How it works

  1. We match your ZIP code to MassDEP-approved Title 5 System Inspectors, closest first. If you picked one, your request goes to it plus the 2 nearest others. Nobody can pay to be picked.
  2. Each one gets your name, contact details, ZIP code, what you need and your note by email.
  3. You get an email listing who we contacted. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. We only send you septic and home-sale tips and offers if you tick the optional box.

Prefer to choose yourself?

Every town page lists the MassDEP-approved inspectors based there and nearby, with the phone, email or website they publish. Call two or three and compare prices.
