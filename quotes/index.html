Who will receive it

What happens to your info
  • Your name, email, phone if you gave one, ZIP code and any note are shared with up to 3 inspectors so they can contact you.
  • They contact you directly. We don't call or text you.
  • Their names appear on the next screen.
  • We keep a copy to run the service. Routing records (a request ID, the date and time, state, the first three digits of your ZIP, the service and who was contacted) are kept for about 13 months.
  • We don't sell your details. Tips and offers only if you tick the optional box.
  • Ask us to delete our copy anytime: hello@title5roster.com. Inspectors keep what they received under their own policies.

How it works

  1. We connect you with up to 3 nearby MassDEP-approved Title 5 inspectors for your ZIP, the nearest ones this form can include. If you picked one, that inspector is included plus up to 2 others. Nobody can pay to be included.
  2. They receive your name, contact details, ZIP code and your note, and they contact you directly.
  3. You see their names on the next screen. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. Tips and offers only if you tick the optional box.
