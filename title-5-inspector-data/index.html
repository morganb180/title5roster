How we count

  • Inspectors vs listings. Each approval on the NEIWPCC list is one System Inspector (SI number). We group inspectors who share a business into one listing, so there are fewer listings than inspectors.
  • County. Comes from the listing's business address. Inspectors travel, so this shows where they are based, not every county they cover.
  • Expiry. Each inspector's approval expiry date as printed on the NEIWPCC list.

Frequently asked questions

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are there?

The NEIWPCC lists dated October 2, 2026 hold 1,139 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors in our records: 1,088 with a Massachusetts address and 51 based outside the state.

Which county has the most Title 5 inspectors?

By business address, Worcester County has the most (188 approved inspectors).

When do System Inspector approvals expire?

Most approvals in our records expire on June 30 of a given year. June 30, 2028 is the most common date (650 inspectors).

Can I download the data?

Yes. The county table is a CSV on this page, and every listing is in api/firms.json.
