Title 5 septic inspectors in Edgartown, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Edgartown? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Edgartown Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 4 approved inspectors with an Edgartown address and 6 more listings elsewhere in Dukes County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Edgartown, MA?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Edgartown Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Edgartown Board of Health.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Edgartown?

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.

How do I check that a Title 5 inspector is approved?
