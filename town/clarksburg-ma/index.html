Title 5 septic inspectors in Clarksburg, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Clarksburg? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Clarksburg Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 1 approved inspector with a Clarksburg address and 54 more listings elsewhere in Berkshire County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

1 MassDEP-approved System Inspector lists a Clarksburg address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Berkshire County inspectors below may also cover Clarksburg. Ask each one about availability and price.
