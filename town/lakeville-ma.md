# Title 5 septic inspectors in Lakeville, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Lakeville? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Lakeville Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 9 approved inspectors with a Lakeville address (8 listings) and 152 more listings elsewhere in Plymouth County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

**9 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Lakeville address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list, grouped into 8 listings. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Plymouth County inspectors below may also cover Lakeville. Ask each one about availability and price.
