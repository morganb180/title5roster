# Title 5 septic inspectors in Edgartown, MA

**4 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Edgartown address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Dukes County inspectors below may also cover Edgartown. Ask each one about availability and price.

Selling a home with a septic system in Edgartown? Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Edgartown Board of Health ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/buying-or-selling-property-with-a-septic-system)). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Edgartown Board of Health.
