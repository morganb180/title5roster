# Title 5 septic inspectors in Monson, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Monson? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Monson Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 2 approved inspectors with a Monson address and 47 more listings elsewhere in Hampden County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

**2 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Monson address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Hampden County inspectors below may also cover Monson. Ask each one about availability and price.
