# Title 5 septic inspectors in Rowe, MA

**1 MassDEP-approved System Inspector** list a Rowe address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Franklin County inspectors below may also cover Rowe. Ask each one about availability and price.

Selling a home with a septic system in Rowe? Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Rowe Board of Health ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/buying-or-selling-property-with-a-septic-system)). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Rowe Board of Health.
