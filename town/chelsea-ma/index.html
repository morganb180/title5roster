Title 5 septic inspectors in Chelsea, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Chelsea? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Chelsea Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 1 approved inspector with a Chelsea address and 4 more listings elsewhere in Suffolk County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Chelsea, MA?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Chelsea Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Chelsea Board of Health.

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Chelsea?

1 MassDEP-approved System Inspector lists a Chelsea address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. 4 more listings are elsewhere in Suffolk County. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability and price.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Chelsea?

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.

How do I check that a Title 5 inspector is approved?

Listings here come from NEIWPCC's list of MassDEP-approved System Inspectors, and each listing links its source. Check the inspector's name on that list before you hire.
