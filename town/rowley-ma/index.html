Title 5 septic inspectors in Rowley, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Rowley? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Rowley Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 2 approved inspectors with a Rowley address and 72 more listings elsewhere in Essex County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

2 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors list a Rowley address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Essex County inspectors below may also cover Rowley. Ask each one about availability and price.
