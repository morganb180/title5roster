Title 5 septic inspectors in Goshen, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Goshen? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Goshen Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 1 approved inspector with a Goshen address and 33 more listings elsewhere in Hampshire County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Goshen, MA?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Goshen Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Goshen Board of Health.

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Goshen?

1 MassDEP-approved System Inspector lists a Goshen address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. 33 more listings are elsewhere in Hampshire County. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability and price.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Goshen?

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.
