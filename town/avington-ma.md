# Title 5 septic inspectors in Avington, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Avington? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Avington Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 1 approved inspector with an Avington address. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

**1 MassDEP-approved System Inspector** lists an Avington address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so inspectors in nearby towns (see the [county pages](https://title5roster.com/counties/)) may also cover it. Ask each one about availability and price.
