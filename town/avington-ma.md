# Title 5 septic inspectors in Avington, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Avington? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Avington Board of Health.
