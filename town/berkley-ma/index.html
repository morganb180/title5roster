Title 5 septic inspectors in Berkley, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Berkley? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Berkley Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 4 approved inspectors with a Berkley address and 103 more listings elsewhere in Bristol County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

4 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors list a Berkley address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Bristol County inspectors below may also cover Berkley. Ask each one about availability and price.
