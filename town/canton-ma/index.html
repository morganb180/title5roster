Title 5 septic inspectors in Canton, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Canton? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Canton Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 2 approved inspectors with a Canton address and 69 more listings elsewhere in Norfolk County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Canton, MA?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Canton Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Canton Board of Health.

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Canton?

2 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors list a Canton address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. 69 more listings are elsewhere in Norfolk County. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability and price.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Canton?

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.
