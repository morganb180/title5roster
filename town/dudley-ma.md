# Title 5 septic inspectors in Dudley, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Dudley? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Dudley Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 3 approved inspectors with a Dudley address and 182 more listings elsewhere in Worcester County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

**3 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Dudley address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Worcester County inspectors below may also cover Dudley. Ask each one about availability and price.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Dudley, MA?**

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Dudley Board of Health ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/buying-or-selling-property-with-a-septic-system)). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Dudley Board of Health.

**How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Dudley?**

3 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors list a Dudley address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. 182 more listings are elsewhere in Worcester County. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability and price.

**How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Dudley?**

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See [sources and what changes the price](https://title5roster.com/title-5-inspection-cost/).

**How do I check that a Title 5 inspector is approved?**

Listings here come from [NEIWPCC's list of MassDEP-approved System Inspectors](https://neiwpcc.org/our-programs/wastewater/mass-title-5/listings-of-approved-system-inspectors-and-soil-evaluators/), and each listing links its source. Check the inspector's name on that list before you hire.
