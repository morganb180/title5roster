# Title 5 septic inspectors in Seekonk, MA

**3 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Seekonk address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Bristol County inspectors below may also cover Seekonk. Ask each one about availability and price.

Selling a home with a septic system in Seekonk? Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Seekonk Board of Health ([Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/guides/buying-or-selling-property-with-a-septic-system)). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Seekonk Board of Health.
