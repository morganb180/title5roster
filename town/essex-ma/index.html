Title 5 septic inspectors in Essex, MA

Quick answer: Selling a home with a septic system in Essex? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Essex Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 3 approved inspectors with an Essex address and 71 more listings elsewhere in Essex County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Title 5 inspection to sell a house in Essex, MA?

Title 5 generally requires an inspection within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Essex Board of Health (Mass.gov). Local bylaws can add rules, so check with the Essex Board of Health.

How many approved Title 5 inspectors are in Essex?

3 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors list an Essex address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. 71 more listings are elsewhere in Essex County. Inspectors often work across a region, so ask each one about availability and price.

How much does a Title 5 inspection cost in Essex?

We don't have town-specific price data. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900, and pumping is often extra. MassDEP doesn't regulate fees, so get written prices from two or three approved inspectors. See sources and what changes the price.

How do I check that a Title 5 inspector is approved?

Listings here come from NEIWPCC's list of MassDEP-approved System Inspectors, and each listing links its source. Check the inspector's name on that list before you hire.
