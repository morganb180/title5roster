# Title 5 septic inspectors in Great Barrington, MA

**Quick answer:** Selling a home with a septic system in Great Barrington? Title 5 generally requires an inspection by a MassDEP-approved System Inspector within 2 years before the sale (3 years if the system was pumped every year), and the report goes to the Great Barrington Board of Health. Title 5 Roster lists 6 approved inspectors with a Great Barrington address and 49 more listings elsewhere in Berkshire County. Published 2026 estimates put the inspection alone at $300–$900; pumping is often extra.

**6 MassDEP-approved System Inspectors** list a Great Barrington address on NEIWPCC's 10/2/2026 list. Inspectors often work across a region, so the Berkshire County inspectors below may also cover Great Barrington. Ask each one about availability and price.
